How it works

A straight line from client to commission.

Three roles, one shared record, no ambiguity about who confirms what.

01

Rep submits the client

Representatives log in with the credentials your office issued and submit client details from the field. It lands in the ledger as pending.

02

Office confirms payment

Your admin checks the incoming payment against the client record and marks it confirmed — the record can't move forward without this step.

03

Admin approves the record

Only once payment is confirmed can the admin approve the client. The rep sees the outcome instantly on their dashboard.
